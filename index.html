We fix ugly.

The black driveway is the part you see. The part you are paying for is protection: from water, frost heave, oxidation, and the replacement bill they cause.

Why driveways die here

Calgary kills driveways in a specific order.

1
Sun

UV dries the oils out of the asphalt. It fades grey and gets brittle.

2
Cracks

Brittle asphalt cracks. Hairlines first, then wider every season.

3
Water

Rain and meltwater get into the cracks, freeze, and jack them apart. This is the expensive part.

4
The base

Once water reaches the gravel base, the driveway moves and crumbles. No one can seal that. It gets rebuilt.

Maintenance costs hundreds. Replacement costs many thousands. The whole game is timing.
What we do about it
Step one

Hot rubber
crack fill

Every crack gets filled with commercial rubber heated to roughly 400°F, the same material used on highways. It bonds to the crack walls and flexes through freeze-thaw instead of popping out by February. The cold-pour filler from the hardware store does not do this.

Step two

JetBlack
oil-based sealcoat

We spray industrial JetBlack sealer. It is oil-based, so it soaks in and replaces what the sun took out, then seals the surface against water, gas and UV. Most low bids use water-based sealer. It looks the same in July and is gone by next summer.

Heat

Infrared repair

Infrared heat re-melts the existing asphalt so potholes and bad spots weld in seamlessly instead of being patched on top.

Lines

Line painting & lots

Parking lots, strata roads and RV parks. Crisp lines and proper layout for commercial and residential properties.

More

Paving & washing

Full paving and chip seal when a surface is past saving, plus hot water pressure washing and concrete clear-coating.
